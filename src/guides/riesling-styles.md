Germany and Central Europe. Drinking windows: [Riesling](/guides/consumption-windows#riesling).

| Style | What to expect |
|---|---|
| Trocken / Grosses Gewächs | Dry, 11.5-12%+ alcohol, crisp and mineral |
| Feinherb / Halbtrocken | Off-dry: a touch of sweetness, balanced by acidity |
| Kabinett | Lightest Prädikat level; dry to off-dry |
| Spätlese | Later harvest, fuller; dry to moderately sweet |
| Auslese | Very ripe bunches; richer, yellow fruit and honey; dry or sweet |
| Beerenauslese / Trockenbeerenauslese | Sweet dessert wines, berry by berry, often botrytis |
| Eiswein | Grapes frozen on the vine; sweet, piercing |

Sources: Justerini & Brooks, Decanter, Wine Folly, Canterbury Wines, WSET.
